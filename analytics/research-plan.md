
# AP Econ Oasis — Product Analytics Research Plan

## 1. Project Background
AP Econ Oasis is an AI-assisted educational website I created to help AP Economics students access study notes, graphs, and practice materials.

I want to explore how students use this website and how data analysis can help me improve their learning experience.

## 2. Research Objective
Understand how visitors discover and interact with AP Economics learning resources, identify potential usability issues, and make evidence-based product improvements.

## 3. Research Questions
RQ1: Which learning modules and resources attract the most interest?

RQ2: How easily can visitors find relevant learning materials from the homepage?

RQ3: Do mobile and desktop users navigate the website differently?

RQ4: What difficulties do students experience when using the website?

RQ5: After implementing a product improvement, is there evidence of better resource discovery or engagement?

## 4. Initial Observations
Source: Lovable Analytics dashboard, October 31, 2026.
Period: Last 90 days.

- Recorded visits: 76
- Social visits: 39
- Direct visits: 37
- Instagram visits: 25
- Homepage page views: 74
- Mobile share: 42.7%
- Desktop share: 56.0%

Limitations:
- Visits may not be unique users.
- My own visits may be included.
- The analytics measurement changed on October 26, 2026.
- Page view totals alone do not establish navigation paths or conversion rates.
- The current sample is small.

## 5. Hypotheses
H1: Clearer navigation (adding topics) could help more visitors discover study resources.

H2: Visitors may use the website differently on mobile and desktop.

These are hypotheses to investigate, not confirmed findings.

## 6. Metrics to Track
- Homepage-to-resource discovery rate
- Module opens
- Quiz starts and completions
- Returning visitor engagement, if measurable
- Qualitative student feedback

## 7. Research Methods
1. Review existing Lovable Analytics reports.
2. Set up privacy-conscious event tracking.
3. Conduct usability tests with approximately five students.
4. Analyze collected data using Python and pandas when sufficient data becomes available.
5. Visualize findings and identify potential improvements.
6. Evaluate the results of implemented changes.
